Questions

Good to know.

Do I have to count calories?

No. Nouriva focuses on food quality — whole foods, nutrients, and balance. You can see calories and macros if you want them, but the headline metric is your Diet Quality Score.

How do I log a meal?

Take a photo, speak a description, type it, or scan a barcode. AI breaks the meal into components — and you can adjust anything on a quick review screen before saving.

How accurate is the AI?

It's good at identifying foods and portions, and it keeps getting better. You always get a review screen to correct items, servings, or amounts, so your data stays accurate to what you actually ate.

Is Nouriva free?

Nouriva is free to download and try. A premium subscription unlocks unlimited photo logging and deeper, longer-range insights.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. Nouriva can sync sleep, weight, and activity from Apple Health to give a fuller picture of how your nutrition connects to how you feel.

Is my data private?

Your data is yours. It's used to power your scores and insights — not sold. See our Privacy Policy for details.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running a recent version of iOS. Nouriva is an iOS app today.
